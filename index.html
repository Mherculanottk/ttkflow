Privacy and Data Protection Policy

Organization: TTK FLOW  |  Version: 1.0  |  Effective date: November 5, 2026  |  Review: every 12 months or when laws or services change
Data Protection Officer (DPO / "Encarregado" under LGPD): TTK FLOW Owner  |  Contact: contato.mherculano@gmail.com

1. Purpose and scope

This policy describes how TTK FLOW collects, uses, stores, shares and deletes personal data obtained from TikTok Shop sellers who authorize the TTK FLOW application, in compliance with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD - Law 13.709/2018) and TikTok Shop requirements.

2. Our role

TTK FLOW acts as a data processor (operador) on behalf of the TikTok Shop sellers that authorize our application. Sellers remain the controllers of their customers' personal data. We process personal data only on the documented instructions of the seller and for the purposes below.

3. Data we process

  • Seller account data: shop identifiers, shop name, authorization tokens.
  • Product and promotion data: catalog, prices, inventory, discounts and promotions.
  • Order and financial data: order details, amounts, settlement information.
  • Buyer personal data contained in orders (e.g. name, shipping address, phone), only when strictly needed for order management and fulfillment.

4. Purposes

Data is used only to provide the services requested by the seller: product and price management, promotion management, sales and financial reporting, and order fulfillment support. Data is never sold, never used for advertising and never used for purposes other than those requested by the seller.

5. Data minimization and storage location

  • We request only the API scopes and fields needed for the service.
  • Personal data is stored and processed exclusively in Brazil (company computers located in Brazil and servers hosted in a Brazilian data center).
  • Buyer personal data is never sent to external artificial intelligence, analytics or other third-party services. Automated assistance features only receive aggregated, non-personal information (e.g. sales totals, product data).

6. Retention and deletion

  • Buyer personal data is kept only as long as needed for the purpose, and for no more than 90 days after the order is completed, unless a longer period is required by law.
  • When a seller revokes authorization or the contractual relationship ends, all data collected from that seller and their customers is deleted within 30 days, and access tokens are revoked immediately.
  • Deletion is confirmed to the seller upon request.

7. Data subject rights

We assist sellers and TikTok Shop in responding to requests from individuals to access, correct, update, delete or obtain a copy of their personal data, within the deadlines required by LGPD (and in any case within 15 days of receiving the request). Requests can be sent to the DPO contact above.

8. Security

Personal data is protected according to the TTK FLOW Information Security Policy, including encryption in transit and at rest, multi-factor authentication, least-privilege access and continuous updates.

9. Sharing and subprocessors

Personal data is not shared with third parties, except the hosting provider in Brazil that stores it on our behalf under confidentiality and security obligations, or when required by law. Any new subprocessor will be evaluated for security before use.

10. Breach notification

Suspected or confirmed personal data breaches are handled under the TTK FLOW Incident Response Plan. Affected sellers and TikTok Shop are notified without undue delay and within 48 hours of confirmation; the Brazilian Data Protection Authority (ANPD) and affected individuals are notified when required by LGPD.

11. Training and review

Everyone with access to personal data must read this policy before being granted access. The policy is reviewed at least annually.
